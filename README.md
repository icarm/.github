# Institute for Computer-Aided Reasoning in Mathematics (ICARM)

The **Institute for Computer-Aided Reasoning in Mathematics (ICARM)** is an NSF-supported Mathematical Sciences Research Institute located at **Carnegie Mellon University**. ICARM’s mission is to help mathematicians use, develop, and shape emerging technologies for mathematical reasoning—including interactive proof assistants, automated reasoning, symbolic computation, and machine learning for mathematics.

## Mission

ICARM is built around three core objectives:

1. **Empower mathematicians** to take advantage of modern reasoning technologies through training, infrastructure, documentation, and technical support.
2. **Support cross-disciplinary collaboration** between mathematicians, computer scientists, educators, and industry research labs.
3. **Improve access and participation for all**, ensuring that new technologies for mathematics benefit researchers and students from all backgrounds.

## Innovation Engineering

A defining feature of ICARM is its staff of **Innovation Engineers**—technical experts in formalization, automated reasoning, machine learning, educational technology, and multimedia. Their responsibilities include:

- Maintaining and extending key communal resources such as Mathlib.  
- Building tools, automation, documentation, benchmarks, and cloud services for the mathematical community.  
- Providing technical support via online platforms, tutorials, workshops, and site visits.  
- Advising and collaborating with mathematicians on research projects involving AI, automation, or formal methods.

## Scientific Program

ICARM’s scientific activities include:

- **Summer Schools for PhD Students**  
  Intensive two-week programs introducing participants to formalization, automated reasoning, machine learning for mathematics, and reasoning about computational mathematics.

- **Focused Research Workshops**  
  Hands-on week-long workshops—two each year—bringing mathematicians, computer scientists, and Innovation Engineers together to launch new projects.

- **Annual Conference on Computer-Aided Reasoning in Mathematics**  
  A broad community event designed to be the flagship venue for AI, formal methods, and automation in mathematics.

- **Collaborative Research Visits**  
  Individual and group visits that provide access to ICARM’s technical staff, computing resources, and interdisciplinary networks.
